# Prototype Brief

## Hypothesis

Adding a first-class “超能力” entry to the existing KWU sidebar, followed by a dedicated capability catalog and a visually complete cross-border ecommerce research workspace, will make the new product concept understandable without changing the current chat, project or Agent runtime.

## Primary user flow

The user selects “超能力” in the sidebar, lands on “选择你的超能力”, opens the available “跨境电商超能力” card, reviews the completed portable-blender research case, switches among dashboard/data/saved views, cites market evidence into the mock composer, submits a deterministic follow-up question, and returns to the capability catalog.

## Success criteria

- The Sidebar exposes an independently selected “超能力” entry in the current Creation layout and remains usable when collapsed.
- The first page clearly says “选择你的超能力” and presents an actionable “跨境电商超能力” card plus visible future-capability placeholders.
- The ecommerce workspace preserves the reference hierarchy: research conversation, task progress, evidence workspace, docked follow-up composer and research capability launcher.
- Dashboard, data and saved-product tabs have distinct usable states.
- Evidence citation creates a removable composer reference and deterministic follow-up response.
- The surface remains usable at 1280×720 and at a narrower desktop viewport without introducing application-level horizontal scrolling.

## Fidelity and data mode

Visual fidelity is medium-high against the transferred screenshots and recording analysis while respecting KWU tokens and shell. Interaction fidelity is medium and local-only. All research content is deterministic Mock data; the prototype does not call the Go bridge or any external service.

## Out of scope

Real model execution, MCP or ecommerce data providers, authentication, durable research cases, persistence, export, collaboration, production routing, backend contracts, mobile-specific design and accessibility certification are outside this version.
