# Prototype Brief

## Hypothesis

A headerless, navigation-free research canvas will feel like an adaptive AI output surface rather than a fixed analytics product, while preserving card-level evidence actions and panel collapse.

## Primary user flow

Open the completed research scenario and review the generated research widgets directly in the right-hand canvas. Cite evidence from a generated widget or collapse and restore the canvas without encountering fixed workspace metadata or view tabs.

## Success criteria

- The right panel contains no fixed title, marketplace badge, or dashboard/data/saved navigation.
- Generated research widgets begin at the top of the panel and retain the existing visual hierarchy.
- Evidence citation remains available from the relevant generated widget.
- The panel can still collapse and restore through unobtrusive edge controls.
- Empty-input mode keeps the generated-component canvas blank.

## Fidelity and data mode

High-fidelity local interaction with deterministic widget specifications standing in for future AI-generated component payloads.

## Out of scope

Model-authored component schemas, runtime code generation, sandbox execution, persistence, and remote data providers remain out of scope.
