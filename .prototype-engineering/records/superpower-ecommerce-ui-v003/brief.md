# Prototype Brief

## Hypothesis

The “空输入” demo is only useful if it represents the true pre-research workspace rather than an empty composer attached to completed research history.

## Primary user flow

Choose “空输入” from the floating demo control and inspect a workspace with no messages, research execution, conclusions, evidence, references, or follow-ups; only the capability header, shortcuts, and empty composer remain available.

## Success criteria

- No completed research content is visible in the empty-input scenario.
- No dashboard, product, or saved evidence is visible in the empty-input scenario.
- The conversation area communicates that no research has started without pretending a result exists.
- Switching back to a populated scenario restores its deterministic research and evidence content.

## Fidelity and data mode

High-fidelity empty-state presentation using deterministic local scenario switching.

## Out of scope

Real research submission, service calls, persistence, and production empty-state copy remain out of scope.
