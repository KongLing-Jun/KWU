# Prototype Brief

## Hypothesis

The ecommerce workspace will feel more like a native chat workspace when persistent product identity and back navigation live in the conversation header, prototype controls float outside the normal layout, and the evidence panel can collapse without disturbing the conversation.

## Primary user flow

Enter “超能力”, open the ecommerce capability, review the consolidated conversation header, optionally switch a deterministic demo state from a floating pill, use the prompt shortcuts above the empty composer, and collapse or restore the evidence workspace.

## Success criteria

- The former full-width ecommerce top bar is removed without losing back navigation or product identity.
- Research shortcuts render above the composer and the composer has no instructional placeholder text.
- The demo selector is a floating pill/popover and includes an empty-input scenario.
- The evidence workspace can collapse and restore while the conversation uses the released width.
- Existing dashboard, data, saved-product, citation, and follow-up interactions remain functional.

## Fidelity and data mode

High-fidelity UI behavior within the existing desktop shell, backed only by deterministic in-memory fixtures. No external services or persistence are introduced.

## Out of scope

Real ecommerce data, model execution, persistence, export, production analytics, permissions, and final mobile navigation remain out of scope.
